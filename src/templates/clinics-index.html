Which clinic should I attend?

When you book, our reception team will confirm which clinic your ophthalmologist consults from and which is most convenient for you. Surgery is performed at South Brisbane Day Hospital, co-located with the Woolloongabba clinic.

Arriving for your appointment

Please arrive 10 minutes early with your referral, Medicare and health fund cards, current glasses, a list of medications and sunglasses, as your eyes may be dilated. Read preparing for your visit for the full checklist.
