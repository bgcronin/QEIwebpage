What we do here

Our ophthalmologists provide medical and surgical management of routine and complex eye conditions, including cataract, cornea and anterior segment, retina and macula, glaucoma, neuro-ophthalmology, oculoplastics and laser eye surgery. The optometrist-led Dry Eye Clinic offers fast, referral-free access to dry eye treatment.
