Dry eye? See our dedicated clinic

Dry eye disease is one of the most common reasons people see an eye specialist. QEI runs an optometrist-led Dry Eye Clinic with advanced treatments such as intense pulsed light, quantum molecular resonance and low level light therapy. No referral is needed and appointments are readily available.
