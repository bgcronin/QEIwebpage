# Initial source inventory

Captured 20 August 2026 from the public navigation of `https://qei.org.au/`.

The initial site scope includes:

- QEI Clinics: Woolloongabba and Clayfield
- Patient information, online enquiry, registration, medical-record request, care feedback, and assessment booking pages
- Ophthalmologist directory and individual profiles
- Eye-condition information
- South Brisbane Day Hospital and Queensland Electro-Diagnostic & Imaging Centre service pages
- QEI Foundation, annual reports, newsletters, donations, bequests, patient stories, and news
- Research, researchers, themes, groups, publications, affiliates, and clinical trials
- Education, optometry education, placements, and introductory ophthalmology resources
- Public news and contact pages

`qeilaser.com.au`, `sbdh.com.au`, clinicians' individual domains, third-party booking services, social networks, mapping services, and other separate domains are external dependencies rather than subdomains of `qei.org.au`; they are not mirrored by default.

The automated workflow additionally performs passive Certificate Transparency discovery for public HTML hosts beneath `qei.org.au`. It deliberately excludes common mail, login, administration, development, test, VPN, API, and internal host labels.
