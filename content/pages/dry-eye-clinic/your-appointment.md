## Booking is simple

Call **07 3239 5000** and ask for the Dry Eye Clinic, or [send an enquiry online](https://qei.org.au/patient-info/send-enquiry/). Because the clinic is led by optometrists, **no referral is needed** and appointments are readily available. If your GP or optometrist has referred you, bring the referral with you.

## Before you come

- Bring all eye drops, ointments and supplements you use, plus a list of your medicines.
- Bring your glasses, contact lenses and your Medicare and health fund cards.
- Avoid eye make-up on the day, and leave contact lenses out if you can.
- Think about when your symptoms are worst, what makes them better or worse, and what you have already tried.

## Your assessment

Your optometrist will take a detailed history and ask you to complete a short symptom questionnaire, then examine your eyes under the microscope. The assessment looks at the quality and stability of your tear film, the health of the surface of the eye, your eyelid margins and lashes, and the structure and function of the meibomian glands.

The aim is to identify what type of dry eye you have, whether evaporative, aqueous-deficient or mixed, and which contributing factors, such as blepharitis, Demodex, allergy or medications, need attention.

## Your plan and costs

You will leave with a clear explanation of the findings and a written plan. Where in-clinic treatments are recommended, we explain what each involves, how many sessions are likely and what they cost, so you can decide in your own time. Medicare rebates may apply to consultations; in-clinic procedures are generally not covered by Medicare, though some private health extras contribute. See [fees, Medicare and referrals](/patient-info/fees-medicare-and-referrals/).

## If we find something else

Dry eye symptoms can occasionally be caused by conditions that need an ophthalmologist, such as corneal disease or inflammation inside the eye. If so, we arrange the referral directly to the appropriate QEI specialist, and your assessment findings travel with you.
