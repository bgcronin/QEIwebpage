## How BlephEx works

The eyelid margins host a thin film of bacteria and debris that builds up over years, especially in people with blepharitis. Bacteria within this biofilm release toxins that inflame the lids and block the meibomian glands, and Demodex mites feed and breed within it.

BlephEx is a handheld device with a soft, single-use medical-grade micro-sponge that spins gently along the edge of the eyelids and the base of the lashes. It exfoliates the biofilm, scurf and mite material, leaving the lid margin clean so that the glands can function and your home care can keep them that way.

## What happens during a session

1. Numbing drops are placed in each eye and you keep your eyes closed.
2. Your optometrist works the spinning sponge along the upper and lower lid margins of each eye. It feels like a gentle tickle.
3. The lids are rinsed. The whole procedure takes about ten minutes.
4. You can go straight back to your day. Mild redness for an hour or so is normal.

## Combining BlephEx with other treatments

BlephEx is frequently the first step in a plan that includes [Demodex treatment](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/demodex-treatment/), [IPL](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/ipl-optilight/) or [low level light therapy](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/low-level-light-therapy/). It is also recommended before cataract and laser eye surgery to reduce the risk of infection and improve healing.
