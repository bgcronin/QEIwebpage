## Why gland expression matters

Each eyelid contains dozens of meibomian glands that release a thin oil onto the tear film with every blink. The oil slows evaporation and keeps the surface smooth for clear vision. When the oil thickens and the gland openings become capped, the tears evaporate quickly, the eyes sting and burn, and vision fluctuates.

Expression physically clears the blocked glands. Once the stagnant oil is removed the glands can resume normal secretion, and treatments such as [IPL](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/ipl-optilight/) and [low level light therapy](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/low-level-light-therapy/) help keep them clear.

## What happens during the procedure

1. The eyelids are warmed to soften the oil, usually by the light-based treatment you have just had or with a heated eye mask.
2. Anaesthetic drops are placed in the eyes.
3. Under magnification, your optometrist applies gentle pressure to the eyelids with a specialised paddle or forceps, working along the lid to clear each gland.
4. The eyes are rinsed and you can carry on with your day.

## Keeping the glands healthy

Between visits, a daily routine of warm compresses, gentle lid massage and lid hygiene keeps the oil flowing. Your optometrist will tailor this routine and review your glands at each visit.
