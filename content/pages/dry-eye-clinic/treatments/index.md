## Building your treatment plan

No two dry eyes are the same. After your assessment your optometrist will explain which of these treatments are likely to help, in what order, and what you can do at home. You will receive a written plan and a quote before any in-clinic course begins.

Home care remains the foundation of good dry eye control: preservative-free lubricants, warm compresses, lid hygiene, omega-3 rich foods, screen breaks and managing your environment. In-clinic treatments make that routine far more effective by addressing the underlying gland and eyelid problems that drops alone cannot fix.
