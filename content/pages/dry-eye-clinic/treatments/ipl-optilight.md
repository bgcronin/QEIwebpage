## How IPL helps dry eye

Meibomian gland dysfunction is the most common cause of dry eye. The oil glands along the eyelid margins become blocked and inflamed, the oil layer of the tear film breaks down and the tears evaporate too quickly. Inflammation of the skin and tiny abnormal blood vessels around the eyelids, common in people with rosacea, keeps the cycle going.

Intense pulsed light delivers precisely controlled pulses of light to the skin below and around the eyes. The light is absorbed by the abnormal blood vessels, closing them down and reducing the flow of inflammatory chemicals to the eyelids. It also gently warms the eyelid glands, helps to liquefy thickened oil, and reduces the load of bacteria and Demodex mites on the lids.

## Why OptiLight

OptiLight by Lumenis uses patented technology that delivers light in a controlled, uniform way, so each pulse is consistent and comfortable. It was the first IPL device to receive US Food and Drug Administration approval specifically for the management of dry eye disease due to meibomian gland dysfunction, following clinical trials showing improved gland function and tear film stability. It is one of the technologies that makes the QEI Dry Eye Clinic among the best equipped in Australia.

## What happens during a session

1. Your optometrist confirms IPL is suitable for you and photographs your eyelids so progress can be tracked.
2. Protective shields are placed over your closed eyes and a cooling gel is applied to the skin.
3. A series of light pulses is delivered across the cheeks and around the eyes. Each pulse feels like a brief warm snap.
4. Most sessions finish with [meibomian gland expression](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/meibomian-gland-expression/), when the warmed glands are gently cleared.
5. You can return to normal activities straight away. Use sunscreen and avoid direct sun exposure on the treated skin for a few days.

## Combining IPL with other treatments

IPL is frequently combined with [low level light therapy](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/low-level-light-therapy/) in the same visit, and with [BlephEx](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/blephex/) when blepharitis or Demodex is present. Your home routine of warm compresses, lid hygiene and preservative-free lubricants keeps the glands healthy between visits.
