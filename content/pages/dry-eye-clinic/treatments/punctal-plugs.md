## How punctal plugs work

Tears drain away from the eye through a small opening, the punctum, in the inner corner of each eyelid. When the eye does not produce enough tears, blocking this drain keeps the available tears on the eye for longer, improving comfort and protecting the surface of the eye.

Plugs are made of soft silicone or of collagen that dissolves over time. They are about the size of a grain of rice and sit within the punctum, where they are not visible and cannot normally be felt.

## What happens during the procedure

1. Your optometrist measures the punctum to choose the correct plug size.
2. Anaesthetic drops are placed in the eye.
3. The plug is inserted with a fine applicator. This takes a few seconds per eyelid.
4. You can return to normal activities straight away.

## When we recommend plugs

Plugs are most helpful when your assessment shows reduced tear production rather than rapid evaporation. Because they do not fix inflammation or gland blockage, they are often combined with [prescription anti-inflammatory therapy](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/prescription-therapy/) or [QMR](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/qmr-rexon-eye/) for the best result.
