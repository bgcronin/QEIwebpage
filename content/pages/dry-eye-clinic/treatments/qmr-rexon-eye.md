## How QMR works

Quantum molecular resonance delivers low-power electrical fields at very high frequencies through soft electrodes in a comfortable mask placed over your closed eyes. The energy is non-thermal, so there is no heat or discomfort, but at the cellular level it stimulates natural repair and regeneration in the lacrimal glands that make the watery part of your tears and the meibomian glands that make the oil layer.

Because it works on tear production as well as tear quality, QMR is particularly valuable for people with **aqueous-deficient dry eye**, including many people with autoimmune conditions or dry eye after eye surgery, and for those with mixed dry eye who have not improved with other treatments.

## What happens during a session

1. Your optometrist confirms the treatment is suitable and records your baseline measurements.
2. You sit back in a reclined chair and the soft Rexon-Eye mask is placed over your closed eyes.
3. The device runs a programmed treatment cycle of about 20 minutes. Most people relax or listen to music; some feel a gentle tingling.
4. There is nothing to recover from. You can drive, work and wear contact lenses afterwards.

## Part of a complete plan

QMR is often combined with [IPL](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/ipl-optilight/) or [low level light therapy](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/low-level-light-therapy/) when meibomian gland dysfunction is also present, and with [punctal plugs](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/punctal-plugs/) or [prescription therapy](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/prescription-therapy/) for more severe aqueous deficiency.
