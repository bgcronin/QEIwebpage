## How low level light therapy works

Photobiomodulation uses specific wavelengths of light, absorbed by the energy-producing structures inside cells, to increase cellular activity and blood flow. In the eyelids, the gentle red light of the Eye-light mask raises the temperature of the meibomian glands, softens and mobilises thickened oil, and encourages the glands to secrete a healthy oil layer that keeps tears from evaporating.

The light is also used to help resolve styes and chalazia (blocked, swollen glands) and to settle the inflammation of blepharitis.

## What happens during a session

1. Your optometrist positions the light mask a few centimetres from your face, over your closed eyes.
2. The mask runs a 15-minute programme. You feel a mild, pleasant warmth.
3. Where indicated, the warmed glands are gently expressed afterwards to clear blockages.
4. You can return to normal activities immediately.

## Who benefits most

LLLT is particularly useful for people with meibomian gland dysfunction who cannot have [IPL](/dry-eye-clinic/treatments/ipl-optilight/), such as those with darker skin tones, and as an add-on for people who have IPL. It is also a comfortable option for teenagers and children with recurrent styes.
