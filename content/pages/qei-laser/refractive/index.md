faq:
  - q: "Which laser eye surgery is best?"
    a: |
      There is no single best procedure, only the best procedure for a particular eye. LASIK gives the fastest recovery and suits most people with a healthy, thick enough cornea. CLEAR lenticule extraction avoids a flap and is gentler on the tear film. TransPRK and PRK preserve the most corneal strength and suit thinner corneas or contact sports. The EVO ICL treats high prescriptions and thin or dry corneas without removing tissue, and refractive lens exchange suits people from their mid-40s who also want reading vision. Your assessment tells us which of these your eyes are suited to, and your surgeon will explain the trade-offs honestly.
  - q: "Does laser eye surgery hurt?"
    a: |
      No. Anaesthetic drops numb the eye and most people feel only pressure for a few seconds. After LASIK, CLEAR or ICL surgery the eyes feel gritty and watery for a few hours. After TransPRK or PRK the surface takes three to five days to heal and is uncomfortable during that time, which we manage with drops, a bandage contact lens and pain relief.
  - q: "How long do the results last?"
    a: |
      Laser reshaping of the cornea is permanent, and the EVO ICL and lens exchange are designed to last a lifetime. Your eyes continue to age, however. Presbyopia (the need for reading glasses) arrives in the mid-40s for everyone, and cataract may develop later in life. Around 2 to 5 per cent of eyes have a small residual prescription after laser and may benefit from an enhancement, which is included in our first-year aftercare.
  - q: "Is laser eye surgery covered by Medicare or my health fund?"

## Every procedure, one honest recommendation

Most laser clinics offer one or two ways to correct vision. QEI Laser Refractive offers all of them, so the recommendation you receive is based on what your eyes need rather than on the equipment in the room. Your assessment includes corneal tomography to rule out keratoconus, a ray-traced optical model of your whole eye, tear film and pupil measurements, and a dilated examination of the retina. You are seen by the surgeon who would operate, and if laser is not the right choice for you, we will say so.

## Technology that sets the standard

- **Ray Tracing LASIK (WaveLight Plus).** Our surgeons were the first in Queensland to offer ray-tracing-guided LASIK, in which a digital model of your entire eye, cornea, lens and axial length, is used to design a treatment unique to that eye. In the pivotal clinical study, 94 per cent of eyes reached 20/20 vision or better without glasses and 70 per cent reached 20/16 or better.
- **CLEAR on the Ziemer FEMTO LDV Z8.** The Z8 operates at 2 to 4 nanojoules per pulse, a small fraction of the energy of other lenticule lasers, with integrated optical coherence tomography for precise lenticule placement. The same laser creates our LASIK flaps and the channels for CAIRS keratoconus surgery.
- **TransPRK.** Single-step, no-touch surface ablation in which the laser removes the epithelium itself, giving faster healing and less early haze than older alcohol-assisted PRK.
- **EVO ICL.** The latest generation implantable collamer lens with a central port, approved by the Therapeutic Goods Administration, for prescriptions beyond the reach of laser or for eyes with thin corneas or dry eye.
- **Premium intraocular lenses.** Extended-depth-of-focus, trifocal and toric lenses for refractive lens exchange, implanted with femtosecond laser assistance where appropriate.
