faq:
  - q: "What is the difference between therapeutic and refractive laser?"
    a: |
      Therapeutic laser treats a disease of the cornea, such as scarring, keratoconus or a dystrophy, to restore vision that glasses or contact lenses cannot. It attracts Medicare and health fund contributions. Refractive laser and lens surgery reshape or supplement a healthy eye's optics to reduce dependence on glasses and contact lenses, and are self-funded. The same surgeons, technology and hospital are used for both.
  - q: "Which service should I contact?"
    a: |
      If you have been told you have a corneal condition, or you have been referred by an optometrist or ophthalmologist for laser treatment of a disease, start with QEI Laser Therapeutic. If you simply want to reduce your dependence on glasses or contact lenses, start with QEI Laser Refractive. Either way, one phone call to 07 3239 5000 reaches the right team.

## One team, two services

QEI Laser is a proud partner of the Queensland Eye Institute, Australia's largest non-government funded eye research institute. Our four laser surgeons are Fellows of the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Ophthalmologists with subspecialty fellowship training in corneal, cataract and refractive surgery at leading centres in the United Kingdom, and they perform every procedure inside a fully accredited private hospital with an on-site anaesthetist.

- **[QEI Laser Refractive](/qei-laser/refractive/)** is for people who want to see clearly without glasses or contact lenses. It offers Ray Tracing LASIK, TransPRK, PRK, CLEAR lenticule extraction on the low-energy Ziemer FEMTO LDV Z8, the EVO implantable collamer lens and refractive lens exchange with premium lenses, so that every eye can be matched to the procedure that suits it best.
- **[QEI Laser Therapeutic](/qei-laser/therapeutic/)** uses the same excimer and femtosecond lasers to treat diseases of the cornea. It is the only excimer laser in Queensland located inside a fully accredited private hospital, which means guaranteed private health fund contributions for therapeutic procedures.

At QEI Laser we are not aiming to be the highest-volume laser clinic in Brisbane; we are striving to be the safest. During our rigorous screening process you will be seen by the surgeon who will be performing your treatment, and we use every technology available to ensure you are a safe candidate.
