## Working together to eradicate preventable blindness

We are passionate about improving our understanding of the nature of eye disease. The institute has a long history of ground-breaking research and collaborates with external partners across the globe so that expertise is shared and our medical research creates real-life impact. The support they provide speaks volumes about the importance of the Queensland Eye Institute and its standing in the community. The best and brightest people work together to make sure we can eradicate preventable blindness in our lifetime.
