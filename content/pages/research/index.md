## Research that reaches patients

Queensland Eye Institute Foundation is Queensland's largest independent academic research institute devoted to eye-related health and diseases. Our laboratories, clinical trials unit and clinics are in one building, so scientists and ophthalmologists work side by side and the questions asked at the bench come directly from the clinic.

We study three major themes, **inherited eye diseases**, **age-related eye diseases** and **renewal and restoration**, focusing on understanding, early detection and best treatment or cure. Our work spans laboratory research, population studies and epidemiology, registries, clinical trials, artificial intelligence and device development.
