## Our purpose

To save sight through excellence in targeted research to reduce the burden of eye disease.

## Three research themes

1. **Inherited eye diseases**
2. **Age-related eye diseases**
3. **Renewal and restoration**, including younger adult eye disease

Within each theme we focus on three areas: **understanding**, **early detection** and **best treatment or cure**, underpinned by scientific excellence and an overall goal of improving eye health.

## Three types of research

- **Investigative research** in the laboratory to understand the mechanisms of disease.
- **Clinical research** with patients to improve diagnosis and management.
- **Clinical trials** of new medicines, devices and interventions.

## Six methods

Laboratory research, population studies and epidemiology, development of registries, clinical trials, artificial intelligence, and development of devices.

To read about our specific research groups and projects, see [research groups and publications](/research/groups-publications/).
