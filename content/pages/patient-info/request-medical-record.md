## How to request your record

1. Open the [online medical record request form](https://qei.org.au/patient-info/request-medical-record/).
2. Enter your details so we can confirm you are the owner of the record, or the guardian of the patient.
3. Choose a standard (last two years) or full record and consent to the release.
4. Pay the fee through the secure payment gateway. QEI does not collect or retain card details.
5. Your record is sent digitally to your nominated email address once your identity has been verified.

## Sharing with another practitioner

If you are transferring your care, ask your new doctor to request the relevant information with your consent. Copies of reports are routinely sent to your referring doctor and, on request, to other practitioners involved in your care.

## Your rights

Access to your health information is a right under the Australian Privacy Principles. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for how we handle your information and how to ask for a correction.
