## New patient registration

Complete the **[patient registration form (PDF)](https://qei.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Patient-Registration-Form-v3-2025.pdf)** and bring it to your first appointment, or arrive ten minutes early to complete it in the clinic. The form collects the details we need to manage your care and bill Medicare and your health fund correctly.

## Other forms

- **[Send an enquiry or referral](https://qei.org.au/patient-info/send-enquiry/)** — new appointment requests and referral uploads.
- **[Request your medical record](/patient-info/request-medical-record/)** — secure online request with payment.
- **[Comment on your care](https://qei.org.au/comment-on-your-care/)** — compliments, suggestions and complaints.
- **[Clinical trial feedback](https://qei.org.au/clinical-trials/feedback-on-clinical-trials/)** — anonymous survey for trial participants.

## Referrers

GPs, optometrists and specialists can use the [online referral form](https://qei.org.au/ophthalmologists/refer-a-patient-form/) or email <a href="mailto:reception@qei.org.au">reception@qei.org.au</a>. See [how to refer](/referrers/).

## Need help?

If you cannot open a form or would prefer a paper copy, call **07 3239 5000** and we will post or email one to you.
