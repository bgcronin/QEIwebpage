## The Australian Charter of Healthcare Rights

Everyone receiving care at the Queensland Eye Institute has the rights set out in the Australian Charter of Healthcare Rights:

- **Access** to healthcare services and treatment that meets your needs.
- **Safety** through care that is safe and of high quality, delivered by qualified and competent staff in a safe environment.
- **Respect** for your culture, identity, beliefs and choices, and for your privacy and dignity.
- **Partnership** in decisions about your care, with the option to include the people you want involved.
- **Information** that is clear and timely about your condition, the options available, their risks, benefits and costs, and your right to ask questions.
- **Privacy** and confidentiality of your personal and health information.
- **Give feedback**, including the right to comment or complain about your care without it affecting the care you receive.

## Tell us how we did

We welcome compliments, suggestions and complaints; they help us improve. You can:

- Speak with any member of our team on the day.
- Complete the online **[comment on your care](https://qei.org.au/comment-on-your-care/)** form.
- Write to the Chief Operating Officer, Queensland Eye Institute, PO Box 8008, Woolloongabba QLD 4102, or email <a href="mailto:info@qei.org.au">info@qei.org.au</a>.

Complaints are acknowledged promptly and investigated by senior staff, and we will tell you the outcome. If you are not satisfied with our response you can contact the **Office of the Health Ombudsman** (Queensland) on 133 OHO (133 646) or at [oho.qld.gov.au](https://www.oho.qld.gov.au/), or the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency for concerns about a registered practitioner.

## Request your medical record

You can request a digital copy of your QEI medical record for yourself or a nominated third party. A small fee covers retrieval, authentication and secure transmission. Identity checks protect your privacy. See [request your medical record](/patient-info/request-medical-record/).

## Your privacy

We collect, store and use your personal and health information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/), which also explains how to access or correct your information.

## Interpreters and accessibility

Interpreters are available free of charge through TIS National on **131 450**. Both clinics have lift access, and our team can assist people with low vision, hearing impairment or mobility needs. Read our [accessibility statement](/accessibility/) for how this website supports larger text and high contrast.
