## How consultations are billed

The QEI Clinic is privately operated. You will receive an account from your ophthalmologist at the end of your consultation, and we ask that accounts are settled on the day where possible. We accept major cards and can process your Medicare claim electronically so the rebate is returned to your bank account promptly.

Clinical tests performed as part of your visit, such as retinal photography, optical coherence tomography (OCT) scans and visual field tests, may be charged separately. Most attract a Medicare rebate.

## Medicare rebates and referrals

To receive a Medicare rebate for a specialist consultation you need a **current, valid referral**:

| Referral from | Valid for |
| --- | --- |
| General practitioner | 12 months from your first visit (or indefinitely if the GP specifies) |
| Optometrist | 12 months, as for a GP referral |
| Another specialist | 3 months |

Bring the referral to your appointment or ask your referrer to email it to <a href="mailto:reception@qei.org.au">reception@qei.org.au</a>. If your referral has expired, your GP can issue a new one, often without an appointment.

## Private health insurance

Private health insurance does not cover consultations or tests in the clinic; these are covered by Medicare rebates. Hospital cover applies to surgery performed at [South Brisbane Day Hospital](/services/southbank-day-hospital/) or other hospitals, subject to your policy, waiting periods and excess. Your fund can confirm your cover if you give them the item numbers from your surgical estimate.

## Concessions

Please tell us if you hold a pension, health care or Department of Veterans' Affairs card, as this may affect billing.

## Uninsured patients needing surgery

Several QEI ophthalmologists hold public hospital appointments. If you do not have private cover, discuss the options with your ophthalmologist, who can advise on public pathways.
