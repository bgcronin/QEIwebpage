## What to bring

- Your **referral letter** from your GP, optometrist or specialist.
- Your **Medicare card**, any **pension or concession card**, and **private health insurance** details.
- A list, or the packets, of **all your current medicines and eye drops**.
- Your current **glasses** and, if you use them, **contact lenses**.
- **Sunglasses**, because your eyes may be dilated and light-sensitive afterwards.
- Any recent **eye reports, scans or photographs** from other practices, if you have them.
- Your completed [patient registration form](/patient-info/forms/), if you are a new patient.

## Getting here

Both clinics are on Level 1 with lift access. See [our clinics](/qei-clinics/) for addresses, parking and public transport. If you have mobility needs, low vision or need an interpreter, let us know when you book so we can help on arrival.

## What happens during your appointment

1. **Check-in.** Our reception team confirms your details and referral and takes a copy of your cards.
2. **Preliminary tests.** Depending on your condition, a clinical assistant or orthoptist may check your vision, measure eye pressure, take photographs or scans of your eye, or test your visual field. Dilating drops may be given at this stage.
3. **Consultation.** Your ophthalmologist reviews the results, examines your eyes and discusses the diagnosis and treatment options with you. Please ask questions; it helps to write them down beforehand.
4. **Next steps.** We book any follow-up, surgery or further tests, provide prescriptions and write to your referrer.

## After dilating drops

Dilation makes your eyes sensitive to light and can blur your vision, particularly for reading, for several hours. **Please do not drive** afterwards. Ask a family member or friend to accompany you, or our reception team can organise a taxi. Public transport is a short walk from both clinics.

## Your personal information

You will be asked to complete a registration form. We collect information about you to manage your care and for administrative purposes such as communicating with your referrer, Medicare or your health fund. Your details are handled in accordance with our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Accounts

The QEI Clinic is a private practice. You will receive an account from your ophthalmologist at the end of the consultation, and we ask that it is settled on the day where possible. Clinical tests performed with your consultation may also be charged; most can be claimed from Medicare. See [fees, Medicare and referrals](/patient-info/fees-medicare-and-referrals/).
