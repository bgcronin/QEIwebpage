faq:
  - q: "Do I need a referral to see a QEI ophthalmologist?"
    a: "To claim the Medicare rebate for a specialist consultation you need a current referral from a GP, optometrist or another specialist. You can still be seen without one, but no Medicare rebate applies. The Dry Eye Clinic is optometrist-led and needs no referral."
  - q: "Where is my appointment?"
    a: "Our ophthalmologists consult at Woolloongabba and Clayfield. Your appointment confirmation will state the clinic. If you are unsure, call 07 3239 5000."
  - q: "Can I bring someone with me?"
    a: "Yes, and we recommend it if your eyes will be dilated, as you should not drive for several hours afterwards."

## We are here to help

Our reception team is available Monday to Friday on **07 3239 5000** to help with appointments, referrals, accounts and general questions. If you need an interpreter, please tell us when you book so we can arrange one through the Translating and Interpreting Service.
