## Three ways to book

<ol class="steps">
<li><h3>Call us</h3><p>Phone <a href="tel:+61732395000">07 3239 5000</a>, Monday to Friday. This is the quickest way to arrange an appointment, especially if your referrer has marked it urgent.</p></li>
<li><h3>Send an enquiry online</h3><p>Complete our <a href="https://qei.org.au/patient-info/send-enquiry/" rel="noopener">online enquiry form</a> with your details and referral, and one of our receptionists will contact you to confirm a time.</p></li>
<li><h3>Book online for selected conditions</h3><p>New patients with certain conditions, such as cataract or pterygium assessments, can <a href="https://qei.org.au/patient-info/book-cataract-or-pterygium-assessment/" rel="noopener">book a new patient assessment online</a>. For any other condition, use the enquiry form or call.</p></li>
</ol>

## Before you book

- **Referral.** To claim your Medicare rebate you need a current referral from your GP, optometrist or another specialist. Ask your referrer to email it to <a href="mailto:reception@qei.org.au">reception@qei.org.au</a> or bring it with you. [About referral validity](/patient-info/fees-medicare-and-referrals/).
- **Details we need.** Your name, date of birth, contact details, Medicare number, health fund (if any), the name of your referrer and a brief description of the problem.
- **Registration.** New patients can save time by completing the [patient registration form](/patient-info/forms/) before arriving.

## Dry eye: no referral needed

If your problem is dry, gritty, watery or irritated eyes, you can book directly into our [optometrist-led Dry Eye Clinic](/dry-eye-clinic/) without a referral, and appointments are readily available.

## Urgent problems

If you have sudden loss of vision, severe eye pain, flashes with new floaters or an eye injury, call us straight away and tell reception it is urgent. Outside clinic hours go to your nearest emergency department. See [emergency eye care](/patient-info/emergency-eye-care/).

## Existing patients

For follow-up appointments, results or prescription queries, call **07 3239 5000**. If your ophthalmologist has asked you to return, we will usually book the follow-up before you leave.
