## Small, regular gifts with a big impact

A simple, effective and convenient way to save sight is to invest in medical research conducted by the Queensland Eye Institute Foundation through workplace giving. Ask your HR department to set up regular payroll deductions to QEIF. This lowers administration costs for our charity and you receive an immediate pre-tax benefit.

For example, $5 per week ($260 a year) could fund 100 experiments to test new drugs that might protect the retina from damaging inflammation, at a pre-tax cost of around $3.50 a week. Ask your employer about matched giving to double your impact: $10 per week ($520 a year) could enable our scientists to take a pinprick of a patient's own eye cells and grow 10 million cells within a month, potentially improving treatment for up to ten patients.

## Benefits for you and your workplace

- Donations over $2 give an immediate tax saving, with no receipts to keep.
- Effortless: your payroll department takes care of it, and you choose the amount.
- A greater sense of purpose at work, stronger employee engagement and a demonstrated commitment to the community.
- Easy to set up and low cost to maintain.

## Partner with us

Our staff can tailor a presentation to explain the work of QEIF and the benefits of workplace giving to your business, and we provide eye health information for your team. Organisations can also support us through volunteering, hosting beneficiary events, fitness challenges, donated products or services, sharing our story through your communication channels, or nominating QEIF as a charity partner.

For more information email <a href="mailto:info@qei.org.au">info@qei.org.au</a>.
