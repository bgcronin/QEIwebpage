## Why the Foundation matters

With no recurrent government funding, the Queensland Eye Institute's survival relies on the generosity of donors, businesses and the community. Every gift supports research equipment, clinical trials of new treatments, eye health education and the training of tomorrow's leaders in eye research. All profits from the South Brisbane Day Hospital are invested back into the Foundation's work.

The QEI Foundation is registered with the [Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission](https://www.acnc.gov.au/charity/c2dd84f6f9c039fba02ce0def3fe0fc2) and is a Deductible Gift Recipient, so donations over $2 are tax deductible.
