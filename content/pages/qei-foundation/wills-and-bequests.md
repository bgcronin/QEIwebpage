## Your achievements can live on with a legacy to vision research

Choosing how to pass on a lifetime's collection of assets, memories and values is one of the most important decisions we make. Including the Queensland Eye Institute Foundation in your will is a simple and effective way to help the fight against preventable blindness. A bequest in your will can fund research or help maintain QEI's advocacy and education programmes.

You don't have to be wealthy to leave a bequest. Everyone can make a difference, no matter how big or small the contribution. Using some of your estate to help the lives of others is a kind and generous gesture, which will be cherished and used wisely. You may not see the impact of your bequest today, but it will have a profound impact on the lives of those who follow in your footsteps, their families and future generations.

## Have the conversation now to save sight tomorrow

If you would like further information on how to include the Queensland Eye Institute Foundation in your will, please call our Bequest Manager directly on **(07) 3239 5050** or email <a href="mailto:info@qei.org.au">info@qei.org.au</a>. We recommend that you seek independent legal advice when preparing or updating your will.
