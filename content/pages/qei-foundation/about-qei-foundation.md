## Our purpose is to save sight

QEIF works every day to save sight by providing innovation and excellence in research, education and clinical care to reduce eye disease, improve eye health, and ultimately eliminate preventable blindness in the community.

Our facilities in Woolloongabba include the QEI Clinic, South Brisbane Day Hospital, the Queensland Electro-Diagnostic and Imaging Centre, medical research and microsurgical teaching laboratories and a medical library, and are home to the QEI Foundation.

## How QEI helps to save sight

- **Clinical care and treatment.** We save the sight of patients every day when they see their ophthalmologist in the QEI Clinic, involving them in cutting-edge research and teaching.
- **Research.** We are saving the sight of future generations through our research and clinical trials.
- **Education.** Saving sight for tomorrow, we educate registrars, fellows, eye care workers and students.
- **Future treatments and cures.** Our research and education lead to new treatments and cures.
- **Donations.** Donations, appeals, bequests and events, together with individual and corporate supporters, help fund QEIF's work.
- **Sustainable funding.** All profits generated from the South Brisbane Day Hospital are invested back into the work of QEIF.

## Tax-deductible giving

The QEI Foundation is a Deductible Gift Recipient. All donations over $2 are tax deductible (not including the sale of raffle tickets, event tickets and other transactions that result in the exchange of goods or services). We provide tax receipts for all valid donations. See our listing on the [Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission website](https://www.acnc.gov.au/charity/c2dd84f6f9c039fba02ce0def3fe0fc2).

## Our history

The Foundation's story began in 1965 with the incorporation of the Australian Foundation for the Prevention of Blindness (Queensland Division), and continues today at our purpose-built home in Woolloongabba. Read the full timeline on the [About QEI](/about/) page.
