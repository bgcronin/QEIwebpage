## Why electrodiagnostic testing matters

The Queensland Electro-Diagnostic and Imaging Centre (QEDIC) provides specialist visual tests that help doctors and ophthalmologists diagnose and treat eye conditions, particularly disorders of the retina and optic nerve. The tests are objective, repeatable and non-invasive, and they provide information about the eye and visual function that is not otherwise available.

They provide critical diagnostic information for conditions that can cause blindness, such as retinal dystrophies, vascular disease, opaque media and trauma, retrobulbar neuritis, unexplained visual loss, vision problems in infancy, toxic and nutritional eye disease, suspected intracranial lesions and undetected cancers, many of which are not identified by conventional testing.

## Tests provided

- Electroretinogram (ERG)
- Pattern ERG
- Multifocal ERG
- Electro-oculogram (EOG)
- Visually evoked potential (VEP)
- Albinism VEP
- Visual acuity (sweep VEP)
- Colour vision (Farnsworth-Munsell)

All tests are carried out in accordance with international standards established by the International Society for Clinical Electrophysiology of Vision.

## A not-for-profit service

QEDIC is operated by the Queensland Eye Institute Foundation and no profit is made from operating the service. It is offered to help those in need, with referrals received from the major hospitals and ophthalmologists in Queensland.
