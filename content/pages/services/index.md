## Integrated care in one place

Our Woolloongabba site brings together the QEI Clinic, the South Brisbane Day Hospital, the Queensland Electro-Diagnostic and Imaging Centre, the QEI research laboratories and the clinical trials unit. Patients benefit from consultation, testing, surgery and research participation without travelling between sites, and our clinicians and scientists work side by side.
