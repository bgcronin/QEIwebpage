## About the hospital

South Brisbane Day Hospital services visiting specialists including ophthalmologists, plastic surgeons and maxillofacial specialists, with five operating theatres and a dedicated procedure room. The hospital has a strong commitment to teaching and research, and staff work closely with clinicians and researchers at the Queensland Eye Institute to improve clinical care and treatment for all patients.

All profits generated by the hospital are invested back into the [Queensland Eye Institute Foundation](/qei-foundation/) to support its sight-saving research. The hospital opened in 2014 as South Bank Day Hospital with the generous support of the Sylvia and Charles Viertel Charitable Foundation, and relocated with QEI to Woolloongabba in 2024.

## For patients

If your QEI ophthalmologist recommends surgery, your surgeon's team will explain the procedure, provide written information and informed financial consent, and the hospital will contact you with admission instructions. Most eye procedures are performed under local anaesthetic with sedation and you go home the same day; you will need someone to take you home.

## Credentialing

Practitioners wishing to practise at South Brisbane Day Hospital should download the Credentialing and Scope of Clinical Practice Application Form and the Hospital By-Laws from [sbdh.com.au](https://sbdh.com.au/). Anaesthetists should also complete the anaesthetist preoperative preferences and preference card forms. Questions about the application process can be directed to the credentialing officer on (07) 3239 5090 or <a href="mailto:credentialing@sbdh.com.au">credentialing@sbdh.com.au</a>.
