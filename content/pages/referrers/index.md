## How to refer

1. **Online:** use the [refer a patient form](https://qei.org.au/ophthalmologists/refer-a-patient-form/) to upload your referral letter and attachments.
2. **Email:** send the referral to <a href="mailto:reception@qei.org.au">reception@qei.org.au</a>.
3. **Phone:** for urgent cases call **07 3239 5000** and ask to speak with the clinical team, then send the written referral.

Please give a copy of the referral to the patient as well; many patients prefer to call us to arrange their own appointment time.

## What to include

- Patient name, date of birth, address, phone number and Medicare number.
- Referrer name, provider number, practice details and a date on the referral.
- The clinical question and relevant history, including duration of symptoms, visual acuity, intraocular pressure and any imaging or visual field results you have.
- Current medications and allergies, and relevant systemic conditions such as diabetes or thyroid disease.
- Urgency, and any preferred ophthalmologist or clinic (Woolloongabba or Clayfield).

## Subspecialty referrals

Our ophthalmologists cover cataract, cornea and anterior segment, retina and macula, glaucoma, neuro-ophthalmology, oculoplastics and orbit, refractive surgery, uveitis and genetic eye disease. Use the [subspecialty guide](/referrers/subspecialty-guide/) to match a presentation with the most appropriate specialist.

## Dry eye: direct access to the Dry Eye Clinic

Patients with dry eye disease, meibomian gland dysfunction, blepharitis or Demodex can be referred directly to our [optometrist-led Dry Eye Clinic](/dry-eye-clinic/), or can self-refer. The clinic offers intense pulsed light (Lumenis OptiLight), quantum molecular resonance (Rexon-Eye), low level light therapy, BlephEx, meibomian gland expression, punctal plugs and anti-inflammatory prescribing, with fast access and no ophthalmology wait. Patients who need ophthalmology input are escalated internally and you are kept informed.

## Electrodiagnostic testing

The Queensland Electro-Diagnostic and Imaging Centre provides ERG, VEP and related tests on referral. Please note the service is currently being redesigned; see the [QEDIC page](/services/qei-electro-diagnostic-and-imaging-centre/) for current availability.

## Education for referrers

QEI runs optometry education evenings, workshops and CPD events throughout the year, and publishes the Introduction to Ophthalmology guide for practice staff. See [education](/education/) and [events](/events/).
