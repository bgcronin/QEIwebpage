## Cataract and lens surgery

Age-related cataract, complex cataract, refractive lens exchange, premium intraocular lenses.
**Specialists:** [Dr Brendan Cronin](/ophthalmologists/dr-brendan-cronin/), [Dr David Gunn](/ophthalmologists/dr-david-gunn/), [Dr Lee Holland](/ophthalmologists/dr-lee-holland/), [Associate Professor Anthony Kwan](/ophthalmologists/associate-professor-anthony-kwan/), [Dr Jonathan Lai](/ophthalmologists/dr-jonathan-lai/), [Dr Alexander Newman](/ophthalmologists/dr-alexander-newman/), [Dr Anthony Pane](/ophthalmologists/dr-anthony-pane/), [Dr Angela Richards](/ophthalmologists/dr-angela-richards/), [Dr Geoffrey Ryan](/ophthalmologists/dr-geoffrey-ryan/).

## Cornea, keratoconus and ocular surface

Keratoconus (cross-linking, topography-guided laser, [CAIRS](/qei-laser/therapeutic/cairs-eye-surgery/)), corneal transplantation including DMEK and DALK, corneal scarring and dystrophies, pterygium, recurrent erosion, severe ocular surface disease. Laser treatment of these conditions is provided through [QEI Laser](/qei-laser/).
**Specialists:** [Dr Brendan Cronin](/ophthalmologists/dr-brendan-cronin/), [Dr David Gunn](/ophthalmologists/dr-david-gunn/), [Dr Lee Holland](/ophthalmologists/dr-lee-holland/), [Dr Geoffrey Ryan](/ophthalmologists/dr-geoffrey-ryan/).
Dry eye disease, meibomian gland dysfunction, blepharitis and Demodex: refer directly to the [Dry Eye Clinic](/dry-eye-clinic/) (optometrist-led, no referral required for patients).

## Retina and macula

Age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy and macular oedema, retinal vein occlusion, retinal tears and detachment, macular hole, epiretinal membrane, vitreous haemorrhage, inherited retinal disease.
**Medical and surgical retina:** [Associate Professor Anthony Kwan](/ophthalmologists/associate-professor-anthony-kwan/), [Associate Professor Abhishek Sharma](/ophthalmologists/dr-abhishek-sharma/).
**Medical retina:** [Dr Alexander Newman](/ophthalmologists/dr-alexander-newman/), [Dr Angela Richards](/ophthalmologists/dr-angela-richards/), [Dr Jonathan Lai](/ophthalmologists/dr-jonathan-lai/).

## Glaucoma

Glaucoma suspects, ocular hypertension, open-angle and angle-closure glaucoma, selective laser trabeculoplasty, minimally invasive glaucoma surgery, trabeculectomy and tube surgery.
**Specialists:** [Dr Geoffrey Ryan](/ophthalmologists/dr-geoffrey-ryan/), [Dr Lee Holland](/ophthalmologists/dr-lee-holland/), [Dr Jonathan Lai](/ophthalmologists/dr-jonathan-lai/), [Dr Alexander Newman](/ophthalmologists/dr-alexander-newman/).

## Neuro-ophthalmology

Optic neuropathies, papilloedema, visual field defects of neurological origin, diplopia, nystagmus, pupil abnormalities, unexplained visual loss.
**Specialists:** [Dr Anthony Pane](/ophthalmologists/dr-anthony-pane/), [Dr Jonathan Lai](/ophthalmologists/dr-jonathan-lai/).
Please note Dr Pane does not accept medico-legal, WorkCover, motor vehicle accident or insurance-related referrals; call for advice on alternatives.

## Oculoplastics, lacrimal and orbit

Eyelid malposition (ptosis, entropion, ectropion), eyelid lesions and cancers, watery eye and nasolacrimal obstruction, thyroid eye disease, orbital tumours and trauma, blepharospasm, socket reconstruction, cosmetic eyelid surgery.
**Specialists:** [Dr Alexandra Manta](/ophthalmologists/dr-alexandra-manta/), [Dr Angela Richards](/ophthalmologists/dr-angela-richards/) (eyelid and tear drainage surgery).

## Laser and refractive surgery

Ray Tracing LASIK, CLEAR lenticule extraction (Ziemer Z8), TransPRK and PRK, the EVO implantable collamer lens and refractive lens exchange through [QEI Laser Refractive](/qei-laser/refractive/); therapeutic excimer laser, cross-linking and CAIRS through [QEI Laser Therapeutic](/qei-laser/therapeutic/). See the [procedure comparison](/qei-laser/refractive/compare-procedures/) and [suitability criteria](/qei-laser/refractive/am-i-suitable/).
**Specialists:** [Dr David Gunn](/ophthalmologists/dr-david-gunn/), [Dr Brendan Cronin](/ophthalmologists/dr-brendan-cronin/), [Dr Lee Holland](/ophthalmologists/dr-lee-holland/), [Dr Geoffrey Ryan](/ophthalmologists/dr-geoffrey-ryan/). Therapeutic excimer laser (topography-guided PTK, PTK for scars, dystrophies and recurrent erosions, the Athens protocol) and PRK/ASA are provided through [QEI Laser](/qei-laser/), located inside South Brisbane Day Hospital; therapeutic procedures attract automatic health fund cover.

## Uveitis and genetic eye disease

Anterior, intermediate and posterior uveitis, inherited retinal dystrophies, genetic counselling pathways.
**Specialist:** [Dr Alexander Newman](/ophthalmologists/dr-alexander-newman/).

## Electrodiagnostics

ERG, multifocal ERG, pattern ERG, EOG and VEP testing through the [Queensland Electro-Diagnostic and Imaging Centre](/services/qei-electro-diagnostic-and-imaging-centre/) (currently being redesigned; check availability).
