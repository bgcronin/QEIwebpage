## General information only

The information on this website, including text, graphics and images, is provided for general educational and informational purposes. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for a consultation with, and the diagnosis and treatment of, a qualified health practitioner who knows your individual circumstances.

Eye conditions and their treatments vary from person to person. Descriptions of procedures, expected results, risks and costs are general in nature; what applies to you will be explained by your ophthalmologist or optometrist during consultation and informed consent. Never delay seeking medical advice, disregard advice you have been given, or stop a treatment because of something you have read on this website.

## Accuracy and review

The Queensland Eye Institute takes care to keep this website accurate and current, and clinical pages are reviewed by our clinicians, but we do not guarantee that the information is complete or free from error, and it does not cover every condition, treatment or circumstance. Pages show a review date and reviewer where a review has been recorded.

## Emergencies

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 000 or go to your nearest hospital emergency department. For sudden loss of vision, severe eye pain, flashes with new floaters or an eye injury, see [emergency eye care](/patient-info/emergency-eye-care/) and contact the clinic on [07 3239 5000](tel:+61732395000) during clinic hours.

## Patient stories and news

Patient stories describe individual experiences; results vary between people and are not a guarantee of any outcome. News articles reflect the date they were published and may not describe current practice.

## Links to other websites

Links to external websites, including clinical trial registries, professional bodies and partner organisations, are provided for convenience. The Queensland Eye Institute is not responsible for their content.

## Contact

Questions about the information on this website can be sent to <a href="mailto:info@qei.org.au">info@qei.org.au</a>. Our doctors do not give medical advice by email; please make an appointment to discuss any medical concern.
