## Our commitment

Many of the people who use this website have reduced vision. We have designed it so that it can be read and navigated comfortably by people with low vision, colour vision deficiency, light sensitivity and by people who use screen readers, keyboards or voice control. Our aim is conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## Display options

Use the **Accessibility** button at the top of every page to:

- **Increase the text size** in four steps, up to 150 per cent, without breaking the layout.
- **Switch to high contrast** (black text on white with strongly underlined links).
- **Choose a light or dark colour theme**, or let the site follow your device setting. Many people with light sensitivity find the dark theme more comfortable.

Your choices are remembered on your device. Browser zoom (Ctrl or Cmd with +) also works to 400 per cent.

## What we have built in

- A **skip link** to the main content and a logical heading structure on every page.
- **Keyboard access** to every menu, button and control, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
- Text and background colours that meet or exceed the contrast ratios recommended by WCAG.
- **Descriptive link text**, alternative text for informative images and captions for figures.
- Layouts that reflow on small screens and at high zoom without horizontal scrolling.
- Support for the **reduced motion** setting on your device.
- Print-friendly versions of patient information pages.
- Plain-English medical information, with technical terms explained.

## Help in the clinic

If you have low vision, please tell us when you book. Our team can read forms to you, provide large-print information, guide you around the clinic and organise transport home after dilating drops. Interpreters are available free of charge through the Translating and Interpreting Service (TIS National) on **131 450**. Both clinics have lift access and accessible bathrooms.

## Known limitations

Some older documents, such as archived newsletters and annual reports, are PDFs that may not be fully accessible. If you need the information in another format, contact us and we will help. Some forms are provided by third-party services whose accessibility we do not control; our team can complete them with you over the phone.

## Tell us about a problem

If any part of this website is difficult for you to use, please email <a href="mailto:info@qei.org.au">info@qei.org.au</a> or call **07 3239 5000**. We will respond promptly and fix what we can.
