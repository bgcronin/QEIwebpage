## Clinical placements

The Queensland Eye Institute offers clinical placements to optometry and medical students. Students observe consultations, diagnostic testing and surgery alongside our ophthalmologists, optometrists and clinical staff, and take part in teaching sessions.

Placements are strictly limited. For enquiries email <a href="mailto:education@qei.org.au">education@qei.org.au</a>.

## Research students

Postgraduate and honours students interested in a research project with the QEI research team should contact <a href="mailto:info@qei.org.au">info@qei.org.au</a> with a brief outline of their interests and their academic supervisor. Read about [our research groups](/research/groups-publications/).
