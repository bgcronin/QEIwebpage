## About the programme

Education events include presentations and small-group, hands-on workshops covering the latest advances in diagnosis, management and shared care across cataract, cornea, retina, glaucoma, neuro-ophthalmology and oculoplastics. Most events attract continuing professional development (CPD) points from Optometry Australia and are free for qualifying participants.

## Stay informed

Subscribe to the optometry education email list on the [QEI website](https://qei.org.au/education/optometry-education-series/) to receive invitations, or email <a href="mailto:education@qei.org.au">education@qei.org.au</a>. Recent and upcoming events are listed on our [events page](/events/).

## Referring to QEI

Optometrists can refer directly to any QEI ophthalmologist, and patients with dry eye disease can be referred to, or self-refer to, our [optometrist-led Dry Eye Clinic](/dry-eye-clinic/). See [how to refer](/referrers/).
