## Teaching the next generation

Education is one of the three pillars of the Queensland Eye Institute. Our ophthalmologists hold academic appointments, supervise registrars and fellows, teach medical and optometry students, run simulation-based surgical training and publish textbooks used around the world. For enquiries email <a href="mailto:education@qei.org.au">education@qei.org.au</a>.
